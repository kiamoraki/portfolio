description: |
  While the individuated 'I' cohabits with the biological basis of the species (sensory perception, etc.), with the public or inter-psychic characteristics of the mother tongue, with productive cooperation and the general intellect, we must add that this cohabitation is not always a peaceful one. The subject is a battlefield. Not infrequently do pre-individual characteristics seem to call into question the act of individuation: the latter reveals itself to be a precarious, always reversible, result.

  ~Paulo Virno. A Grammar of the Multitude

  Precarity: A Domestic Tale explores the increasingly common experience of precariousness: in labor, in space, in relationships. One way in which we try to combat the inevitable and constant shifts in our lives, our economies, our environments, is to tell stories, to use narrative as a way to fix what is otherwise unpredictable and unstable. This multi-media installation encourages and thwarts the construction of narrative in a temporary (and thus also precarious) occupation of domestic space. Our neighborhoods, our shared streets, our homes and our rooms are also themselves fluctuating forces. They tell unstable stories (and sometimes stories about the unstable) and, of course, we tell stories about them.

  Precarity: A Domestic Tale was installed at the "Rooms to Let: CLE" in Slavic Village, OH. 2015.

description: |
  Curious about the math behind sacred geometry, my research led me to rose mathematics, an algorithm that generates a flower-like polar curve using the equation r = asin(kθ). Small parametric adjustments to the frequency ratio k alter the symmetry, looping, and complexity. These small shifts within a system create variation and a family of forms.

  In isolation, the particles blindly traverse their predestined paths.

  As loops layer into shared frame, possibility and choice present themselves: what could have been, what can be. The trajectories intersect – an opportunity to phase shift into another sequence.

  The work was exhibited in The Portal, an 18-screen video installation constructed off-grid in the desert for Mars College.

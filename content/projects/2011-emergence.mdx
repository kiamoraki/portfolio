description: |
  Emergence explores the dynamics of relationship. Inspired by Conrad's Game, the work depicts energetic bodies moving through fields of attraction and exchange. Rings and points evoke gravitational systems, planetary orbits, and atomic structures, different scales of the same underlying pattern.

  As systems approach one another, energy transfers between them. New patterns arise through interaction, revealing emergence not as a property of isolated objects, but as a consequence of relationship.

  Emergence occurs when relationship produces something neither system could create alone. Complexity arises not from individual parts, but from the space between them.

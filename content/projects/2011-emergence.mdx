description: |
  Emergence investigates relationship as a generative force. Drawing on Conway's Game of Life — the cellular automaton in which simple rules yield unforeseeable complexity — the work renders energetic bodies drifting through fields of attraction and exchange. Rings and points recall orbital mechanics at every scale: the atom, the planet, the galaxy, each an iteration of a single underlying pattern.

  As these systems draw near one another, energy passes between them and new forms take shape — patterns that belong to neither body alone. The work proposes that emergence is not a property of objects but a consequence of encounter: complexity lives not in the parts, but in the space between them.

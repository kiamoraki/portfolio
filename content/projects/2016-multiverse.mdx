description: |
  Multiverse explores the possibility that multiple realities exist simultaneously, each governed by its own center of gravity and internal order. Inspired by theories of cosmic expansion, the work begins with recognizable orbital structures organized around distinct centers. These structures function as self-contained universes, each possessing its own coherence and momentum. As each universe expands outward, their particles overlap and intermingle. As the universes intersect, their individual organizing principles become increasingly difficult to distinguish. Order gives way to ambiguity before eventually reconstituting itself in new forms.

  The work cycles continuously between expansion and contraction, imagining reality as a dynamic process of separation, convergence, and transformation. The work speculates on the possibility that new realities emerge where previously distinct worlds begin to overlap. The piece functions as a metaphor for consciousness, culture and social life. Distinct systems of meaning appear stable and self-contained until they encounter one another. At points of overlap, new possibilities emerge. These intersections are portals of transformation–moments where movements between realities becomes possible, and where entirely new worlds may be formed through encounter.

description: |
  Each life is a temporal sculpture, formed through attention, perception, and choice. What if we are not subjects of time, but artists of its form?

  The Shape of Time is a sculptural exploration of time. Using mathematics to visualize invisible structures, the work transforms time from a measurement into a material. Time is no longer a line to be followed, but a malleable form to be shaped. We are not merely moving through time, we are sculpting it.

  Duration becomes secondary to form and the question shifts from how much time has passed to what shape our lives have created.

<Text>
  <p>I learned how to DJ in 2022, and fell in love. I find comfort in the loud dark, in the equalizing force of a hot sweaty dance floor.</p>
  <p>My third year at Mars, I wanted to explore collaboration. I was surrounded by creatives — world class live music visualists, 3D modelers, and creators building the algorithmic music scene from the ground up. A reformed socially awkward weirdo, I knew I could galvanize people together to co-create. Leading with empathy, compassion, and carrots, I came into the Mars season on a mission to build a tight crew of performers, to create a platform for others' creative expression. No barriers to entry, a safe space to shake off performance jitters and just fucking do it. And we did it. We produced events every full and new moon (a ridiculously demanding schedule). I melded algo raves and proper raves. I started open decks and every single martian participated. It was glorious.</p>
  <p>Everyone&apos;s fam in the club.</p>
</Text>

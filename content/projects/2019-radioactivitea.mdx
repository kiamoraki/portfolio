description: |
  RadioactiviTEA was created as part of Lost Horizons Night Market, an event series produced by Shadow Traffic that supports emerging artists through participatory installations in overlooked and underutilized urban spaces. The project transformed the interior of a box truck into an immersive tea environment, creating an unexpected refuge within an industrial landscape.

  Visitors were transported from a world of concrete, infrastructure, and movement into a lush forest-inspired setting designed to slow perception and encourage connection. The familiar structure of a tea gathering became a vehicle for creating temporary community among strangers. Within this space, efficiency gave way to presence, consumption gave way to participation, and strangers became collaborators in a shared experience.

  RadioactiviTEA functioned as a temporary autonomous zone—an ephemeral environment operating according to a different set of values than the surrounding city. Through hospitality, ritual, and spatial transformation, the installation explored how temporary spaces can create new social possibilities.
